## Method

This is a systems audit based on material that was public by August 3, 2026. It distinguishes documented advocacy work from the absence of a visible movement-wide intake, triage and case-management standard. It does not infer that a group lacks an internal process merely because the process is not public.

The report uses the public programs of NYC Anarchist Black Cross, the Anarchist Black Cross Federation and the Jericho Movement as examples of substantial existing work. Federal prison oversight, compassionate-release and clemency materials establish the procedural and medical conditions that make early preparation necessary.

The recommendations and targets are NPPC policy proposals. They are not presented as existing standards or externally measured facts.
