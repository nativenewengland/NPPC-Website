## Source method and caveats

NARA ID404788077, *Register of Native Prisoners Confined, October 1900–May 1902*, RG395, entry3025, contains 26 digital objects. The original facing-page images for manuscript pages2,3,4 and6 were visually read, with enlarged details for overwritten capture/release dates. Images4,5,6 support this batch. The modern Batangas transcription was a discovery aid; names and dispositions here were checked against the primary scans.

Ditto marks carry the capture date, residence, reason and disposition only within their own columns. Monthly repeats of Endozo, Cadacio and Evangelista are one person and one continuous episode each. Endozo/Endoza and Paulin/Paulino spelling variants are aliases, not new people. Sinforoso de Leon is the corrected name above a struck-through reversed spelling. Brigido Miña retains the accent and an unaccented matching alias. Pinagkurusan is a cautious residence normalization; no coordinates are entered.

Ramon Mortel's oath and surgeon wording is struck through, so only the uncancelled release and local-official responsibility are reported. For the Macuha/Miña rows, the clerk replaced the Balayan-president wording with Mariano Ramos. Paulino Evangelista's tax-collector classification follows the original ditto sequence; the January ledger records death in the post hospital on January9 and burial in Balayan. No disease or other cause of death is inferred. The model mirrors his explicit death endpoint into its release-date column to stop the counter, while the profile remains `released=false`.

The register ages are insufficient for exact birthdays. No face has been individually identified in a reusable photograph. No modern institution ID is assigned to an unlocated military stockade. Profiles contain concise accounts of this source, not reconstructed life histories. Existing biographies, fields, cases and images remain untouched.

## Remaining register work

This batch covers selected clear December dispositions and two January outcomes. It does not exhaust the ledger. Several first-page names are held for spelling or altered-date review, including Prudencia Masancay, Apolonio Aranis, Ambrocio de los Reyes, Catalino Cabrera, Felix Paderona and Ramon Hernandez. Others continue into later monthly pages. Follow the original corrections rather than accepting the modern transcription blindly. For example, February image8 appears to retain November27 for the carried-over capture column, while the modern transcription renders November12; do not silently substitute its reading.

Continue with January/February transfers and later months. A transfer to Manila or Calaca is not release to freedom. Carrying an agricultural bolo alone does not establish political detention; compare the individual cause field and subsequent disposition. Exclude ordinary criminal rows. Do not assign group photographs to unidentified individuals.
