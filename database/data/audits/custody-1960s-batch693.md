# 1960s custody review: batch 693

Joseph E. Mulligan: May 25, 1969 arrest; 1970-1972 prison term with year-only precision and documented 24 months served. Initial Cook County Jail detention is not conflated with later Sandstone imprisonment. Exact admission and parole dates remain unresolved. Preserve biography and separate 2003 case.

The Irish Echo participant interview corroborates arrest, sentence and 1972 parole. Mulligan also describes two years in his March 1, 2004 letter.
