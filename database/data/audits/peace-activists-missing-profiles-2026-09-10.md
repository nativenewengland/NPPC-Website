# Peace-activist list: database gap audit

Read-only comparison completed September 10, 2026. Starting list: [Wikipedia — List of peace activists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_peace_activists). Database snapshot: **8,668 prisoner profiles**, including records outside public visibility scopes. No database data was changed.

Follow-up: [batch 267 custody review](peace-activists-multiday-us-custody-batch267.md) applies the user’s multiple-day US-custody threshold. Six new profiles are prepared there. The name totals here include the subsequent correction identifying Ed Sanders as existing Edward Sanders; they describe the database before batch 267 deployment.

## Counts and limits

The page contained 715 bullet entries. Bono appears twice and is also listed under Paul David Hewson; those three entries represent one person. A photograph caption embedded before Jane Valbot was excluded from her name. The resulting comparison covers **713 distinct listed people: 72 with existing name/alias matches and 641 without a match**. These are name-comparison results, not 641 proven missing political prisoners. Unknown aliases, transliterations and incorrect legacy aliases remain possible.

All listed names were compared against name, slug, known aliases and component names, with accent/punctuation normalization and a secondary surname/first-name review. The custody research below is a verified shortlist, not a completed detention investigation of all 641 unmatched people. The list is international and includes people without any known imprisonment. Overseas cases require a decision about the database’s geographic scope before inclusion.

Identity checks corrected several misleading results: Captain Howard Levy is already Howard Levy; musician George Harrison is not IWW prisoner Harrison George; William Sloane Coffin is present and is not the Everett defendant William Coffin; White House vigiler Thomas is not the WWI conscientious objector St. William Thomas. The Coffin legacy name/alias overlap should be reviewed separately, not automatically merged.
