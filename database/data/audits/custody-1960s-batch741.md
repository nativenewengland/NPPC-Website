# Batch 741: Blair Anderson guarded hospital custody

The court describes the arrest and imprisonment of the seven raid survivors on December 4, 1969; the Commission of Inquiry names Anderson among those arrested and held. The December 13 Black Panther report specifically describes Anderson in hospital under armed guard after surgery. Custody began with the raid, including guarded hospitalization, not an inferred later jail transfer. Individual release remains unverified.
