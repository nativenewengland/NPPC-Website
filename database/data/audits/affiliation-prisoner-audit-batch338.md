## Date and identity review

Andres Valencia/Balencia has November 27 capture in earlier rolls and November 24 in March; November 1900 precision retains this conflict. Cirilo Garcia, Cornelio Alaras, Mariano Reyes and Francisco Martinez have February 20/21 capture conflicts and therefore February 1901 precision. Exact March dispositions are retained where legible. No birthdays are reconstructed from ages.

The March 20 escape of Gregorio Riel ends recorded physical custody but is explicitly distinguished from a pardon or authorized release. Explicit parole releases end physical confinement; later reporting conditions are not counted as jail. Where marked, ditto signs carry only the relevant preceding column. Blank cells are not read as ditto signs. Repeated monthly rows are one identity and one custody period.

The March 25 entry naming Crisanto Deocampo concerns robbery and assault; it is not added as political custody or merged with the March 1–10 insurgency detention. No unverified sentence, death date, precise stockade coordinates, modern prison ID, portrait or personal support website is supplied.

**Francisco Martinez is distinct from Francisco “Kiko” Martinez**, the Colorado attorney born in 1946. The Balayan record predates that birth by 45 years. Read-only review confirmed the existing profile describes the attorney and his twentieth-century prosecutions. Batch 338 excludes only existing UUID `7a0a3c07-b1bb-49de-843d-5705e422a6c8` when all six reviewed identity fields remain unchanged. A changed field stops the batch for renewed review. The existing attorney's profile and cases remain untouched. Tests cover this exception, preservation, replay and refusal after an identity change.
