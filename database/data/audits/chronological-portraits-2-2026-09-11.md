# Second chronological prisoner image batch: 100 additions

Added another 100 sourced photographs and historical depictions to 100 live prisoner entries that had empty image fields immediately before this update. This is separate from the preceding 100-entry batch.

## Coverage and continuation

Research resumed from the earlier batch and progressed through the remaining 1918-1929 cohorts into early 1930. Follow-up sources also supplied images for earlier cases. The additions have existing first-case dates from 1917-11-14 through 1930-03-06. Counts by first recorded case year: 1917: 1, 1918: 12, 1919: 29, 1920: 5, 1921: 1, 1923: 1, 1925: 1, 1927: 9, 1928: 11, 1929: 6, 1930: 24.

The 1700s and 1800s review is documented in the preceding batch's 314-entry early-review ledger. This batch used institutional collections, contemporary newspapers and Labor Defender scans, historical books and corroborated biographies. A negative search means no verified usable image was found in the sources inspected, not that no image exists.

Resume with the remaining 1930 entries. The 1930 cohort is not exhausted. Already located for follow-up: Mary Dalton, M. H. Powers and Joe Carr in the July/August 1930 Labor Defender and the named Atlanta Six portrait sheet at https://revolutionsnewsstand.com/2025/04/05/76326/. These three were not attached or counted in this batch because the requested 100 was reached. The July 1930 issue also contains a captioned Wisconsin March 6 defendants photograph worth checking against missing entries. Earlier unresolved cases can be revisited when new source collections become available.

## Sources and treatment

[Credits and identity evidence for every addition](../photos/CREDITS-chronological-portraits-2-2026-09-11.md), [machine-readable provenance](../fixes/chronological-portraits-2-2026-09-11.json), [addition ledger](chronological-portraits-2-2026-09-11-additions.csv), and [retained research logs](chronological-portraits-2-2026-09-11-queries.json).

Original source files, checksums, dimensions and exact crop coordinates are retained. Only original downloads, ordinary lossless format conversion, right-angle orientation correction and rectangular crops were used. No AI generation, enhancement, restoration or retouching. The Martha Foley and Steve Kurepa entries use historical illustrations; some other newspaper portraits are engraved or heavily screened reproductions. Small or faded images retain their original limitations. The Einosuke Yamaguchi image retains the whole jail-departure scene and handwritten caption because individual face positions were not separately identified. Rights and credits are recorded individually; uncertain rights are not represented as confirmed public domain.

The final review checked all 100 finished images and corrected the Newark row positions for Edward Childs and Albert Hedar before publication. It also tightened the Angelo Cappelo, Albert Hedar and Jessica Henderson crops. No incorrect draft crop was applied to the live database.
