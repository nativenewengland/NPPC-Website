# Panther prisoner research — batch 266

Prepared September 9, 2026. The preceding audit reviewed 88 mapped chapter/organizing locations against 8,614 database profiles. This batch contains **54 new profiles, 54 associated cases, one earlier case for existing prisoner Veronza Bowers, 12 photographs, and eight birth/death date fields across six people**. It does not certify that every chapter member or detention has been identified.

Of the original 116 candidates, 47 stronger candidates and six formerly uncertain leads are included as new people; Daoud Bey was identified as existing prisoner Veronza Bowers. Sandra Wallace is an additional person discovered during follow-up. The other 62 leads remain below for further verification. An arrest report, ticket, party membership, or same-name match alone was not treated as proof of jail confinement. Nate Bellamy is specifically described as hospital custody under police guard, not as serving a verified prison term.

## Identity and evidence decisions

- The Bowers appellate record identifies Daoud Bey as his alias. Batch 266 adds only the documented February 9, 1970 Richmond detention case to his existing record; it does not create a duplicate or alter his biography.
- The original newspaper identifies Gail Noldon, captioned Gail Spillard. The earlier “Grail” spelling is retained only as a duplicate-check alias.
- The 1969 San Francisco Harold Holmes and the existing New Orleans Harold Holmes have not been independently established as the same person. No new Holmes profile or case is included.
- Howard Moore, James Johnson and Charles Oliver have unrelated or unresolved same-name matches. Those leads still require case/custody verification.
- Partial birth/death years remain year-only. Unknown birthdays, death dates, release dates, detention durations and exact charge counts are not invented. Sentences imposed are distinguished from time actually served.
- Photographs were checked against named captions or person-specific archival pages. Exact rectangular crops retain original pixels; no AI reconstruction was used. Some newspaper originals are small or faded. See [photo credits](../photos/CREDITS-batch266.md).

## Preservation and validation

The batch uses Eloquent inside a transaction and performs source, date, identity, photo-format and SHA-256 validation before insertion. It checks all profiles, including hidden records, for name/alias matches. Existing profiles are skipped unchanged; conflicting identities or Bowers cases abort the batch. New records are appended to the existing order. No migrations insert data. Research URLs stay in the payload, not in personal-support website fields. Coordinates are approximate organizing/case city points, not prison addresses.

The live read-only preview reported **54 profiles and one earlier case to add**, with no matching existing profiles among the 54. Production SQLite was placed in query-only mode during this check. No production records or photo assets were changed.

An isolated in-memory SQLite exercise using the application models passed **442 assertions**: dry-run preservation; first application; identical replay; partial-date precision; photo attachment and hashes; unknown duration handling; existing-profile preservation; malformed-input rejection; conflicting-case rejection; and transaction rollback on a simulated photo failure. Photo storage and caches were replaced by in-memory test doubles. Shell syntax validation passed.

```bash
cd /var/www/NPPC-Website

```
