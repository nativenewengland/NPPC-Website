- **Hartman Turnbow:** May8,1963 arrest after his home was firebombed. Sworn testimony reproduced in the1964 Congressional Record says two days jailed before bond. The May21,1963 SNCC newsletter collectively reports release May13. His precise release day is unresolved: store May1963, describe the two-day testimony, and do not assign the five-day co-defendant interval. Later charges dropped; no final arson conviction inferred. SNCC/Duke biography supplies March20,1905 birth and August15,1988 death.
- **Euvester Simpson:** June9,1963 Winona arrest; oral history describes multiple nights and release around Wednesday. June1963 release precision retained. Detailed SNCC Legacy Project/Duke biography supplies December12,1945 birth. Smithsonian collection search-index metadata lists born1946: the detailed biography is the chosen source, and the catalog discrepancy remains visible here for review. No date calculated from age. No death date verified. The oral-history account distinguishes her treatment from the more extensive beatings of fellow prisoners; the new bio does not amplify it. Being in Oxford to testify is not a custody period.
- **Unita Blackwell:** an eleven-day completed Jackson detention is explicitly reported in Peter T.Kilborn's June19,1992 New York Times profile, reproduced in Congressional Record,p.15765. Dates for that particular interval are absent; no invented1965 endpoints. March18,1933 birth and May13,2019 death follow SNCC/Duke. The civil school-integration lawsuit is not entered as a criminal prosecution.
- **John Ball** and **Lavone Hampton:** explicitly named in the contemporary SNCC newsletter's May8 arrest and May13 release report. Only the arson allegation is assigned; Moses's separate interfering-with-investigation charge is not theirs. No verified vital dates or portraits; unrelated John Ball biographies are excluded.
