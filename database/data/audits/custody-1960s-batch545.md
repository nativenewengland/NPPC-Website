# Robert F. Williams: same-day US custody

Contemporary September13 1969 NYT report reproduced on Senate hearing p62 reports arrival, federal arrest/bail, county custody and recognizance release on September12. Court opinion independently confirms arrival September12 and preceding British custody. Replace false continuous September11-December31 US interval with same-day custody. Zero elapsed days does not mean no arrest. Do not fold British detention into US case.
