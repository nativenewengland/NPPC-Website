## Sources for additions

Kelly Postal: https://www.themilitant.com/1945/0903/MIL0903.pdf — The Militant, January 20, 1945, page 1, obituary of Mae Postal, explicitly reports Kelly Postal released on parole May 30, 1944, on condition of moving to Oregon. Applies to state embezzlement imprisonment, not the separate federal acquittal. Admission day remains unknown; no date calculated from nine months.

Ioannis Viglakis: https://www.justice.gov/archive/usao/nys/pressreleases/September14/IoannisViglakisSentencing.php — DOJ September 3, 2014 sentencing announcement names judge, prosecution team and guilty-plea date. August 2012 Panama arrest and subsequent U.S. transfer do not establish the precise U.S. prison admission; left blank.

Dan Schwankl: https://media.nukeresister.org/wp-content/uploads/resister/nr137.pdf — The Nuclear Resister #137, July 29, 2005, printed page 3, SOA Grads’ Abuses Fuel Resistance, explicitly names Dan Schwankl among nine who began serving on March 15. Original PDF retrieved from publisher’s relocated media host, text extracted and page visually checked. Existing June 10 release preserved; admission is not back-calculated from sentence.

José Víctor García Díaz: https://socialistworker.org/2016/05/09/detained-for-organizing-vermont-workers — May 9, 2016 retrospective explicitly says Victor Diaz was released on bail May 4. https://vtdigger.org/2016/05/04/migrant-justice-group-bail-granted-for-activist-facing-deportation/ contemporaneously confirms May 4 grant of $1,500 bail, but describes release prospectively; the later report establishes actual release. Identity matched to full Jose Victor Garcia-Diaz name, Migrant Justice and April 21 detention. No end to removal proceedings or supervision inferred.

Tandre Buchanan: https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndoh/pr/cleveland-man-sentenced-prison-after-robbery-and-evidence-tampering-conviction — March 23, 2022 DOJ report establishes sentence date, judge and trial prosecutor. https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca6/22-3697/22-3697-2023-08-21.html — Sixth Circuit opinion establishes July 23, 2020 indictment and jury convictions. Restitution was partially vacated, so no restitution amount added.
