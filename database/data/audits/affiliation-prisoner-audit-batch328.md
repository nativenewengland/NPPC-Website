- The user specified US custody. This includes custody by the US military abroad; it is not restricted to US soil. Hall's case begins in Georgia county jails and continues under US military authority, including Kuwait. Overseas locations are explicit, not presented as US geography. Transfers are not false releases or new arrests. No coordinates or institution relationships are inferred.
- Agosto's May refusal and proposed July hearing are not entered as custody dates. The actual August5 hearing/removal and August29 release support the recorded interval. Discharge was later and is not substituted for jail release.
- Hall's own account and the detailed charge narrative identify Article134. One transfer update mistakenly says Article132. Alleged threats are not described as proven, and no conviction is inferred from pretrial detention. The April17 update establishes freedom by then, not an exact April17 release. The letter's printed1010 is a typo for2010.
- Clousing's eleven-month sentence included eight suspended months. The support page's December20 header precedes its December23 homecoming narrative. The December month is supported; no exact release day or ninety days actually served is inferred. Earlier surrender is not assumed to be the start of uninterrupted jail custody.
- Wilkerson's eyewitness trial account names unauthorized absence and missing movement; the campaign page calls the plea desertion. The discrepancy is explicit. The campaign's July26 update already places him outside prison; the profile roster's August release is not used. Its nearly-seven-month language does not match February-to-July arithmetic, so sentence length is not treated as a measured term. Exact commitment day remains unresolved.
- Aguayo's April18 release is independently confirmed by Amnesty. The roster's August release is erroneous. Pretrial credit is part of the same custody, not added twice to the sentence.
- IVAW participation is documented for Agosto, Hall and Wilkerson. Clousing and Aguayo receive a broad Iraq War resistance affiliation; formal IVAW membership is not inferred from supporters. Birthdays found only in unsourced summaries and namesake obituaries are withheld. No existing bios or other fields are edited.
