# Sol Harper race correction

Correct race White to Black. Existing biography already identifies him as Black. Contemporary Daily Worker April14,1930 specifically distinguishes Harper, a Black ILD/ANLC organizer, from white co-defendant Rose Kelly. Congressional Record October16,1929 also identifies him as Black. No inference from name or appearance used. The original cause of the erroneous White field has not been established. All other fields, including the recent affiliation removal, preserved.
