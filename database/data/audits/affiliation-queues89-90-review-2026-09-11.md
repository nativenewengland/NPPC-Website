## John Brown Anti-Klan Committee

The June1985 newsletter names Christine Rico, Sandra Roland, Julie Nalibov and Steven Burke in prison, and Terry Bisson and Bob Lederer as the next witnesses subpoenaed. All six already appear in the all-profile inventory. Cameron Bishop also already exists. Bisson's later first-person account confirms his months of imprisonment; the newsletter's prospective May14 appearance must not itself be treated as prison entry.

The original PDF page3 was downloaded, rendered and visually read. Its photograph identifies the four resisters left to right; useful for a later missing-photo audit, subject to checking each existing portrait first. The physical fold obscures parts of the text: Terry Bisson's name is visible in the image although missing from the extracted text. No images assigned during this new-person pass.

Bob Wells and Henry “Camo” Bortman are excluded from the1989 grand-jury lead: the later campaign report says the government backed down before jailing them. Preparation to serve18 months is not actual detention. Laurie Thrasher/MARK received a civil judgment in the newsletter report; no individual multi-day jail period is established there.

These five are not added without separate evidence of actual multi-day custody. The study's custody discussion does not identify Burgum; his existing profile's attribution of the board prosecution requires an individual source check, not deletion or a presumed correction based on absence alone.

The same study provides more precise custody leads for existing records: June7 entry for the eleven, November13 for Bryan/Fleischman, and November7 release for Barsky. These are research leads to compare with primary documents and existing populated fields, not automatic overwrites. No foreign refugees are added merely because they received aid.
