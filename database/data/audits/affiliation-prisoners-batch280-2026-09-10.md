### Cowden

[The March 27, 2014 DOJ announcement](https://www.justice.gov/usao-edmo/pr/local-man-sentenced-computer-hacking-charges) records a **15-month sentence** for computer fraud involving an Israeli-business website. The [January 2016 FreeAnons appeal](https://samidoun.net/2016/01/anonymous-activist-jon-cowden-remains-in-prison-for-his-role-in-oppalestine/) connects his activity to Palestine solidarity and confirms time already served plus renewed custody at **FCI Victorville Medium I**. The latter account's October 2015 arrest and August transfer chronology conflicts internally; no exact return date is assigned. Its projected July 2016 release was independently confirmed through BOP's actual-release field for register number **37215-298**.

Other support pages describe 12, 13 or 15 months and sometimes identify a St. Louis mayor's office attack as the conviction. The official conviction description takes precedence, while the exact served duration remains unresolved. Original custody and later supervision-related confinement are separate cases. The second case describes alleged supervision violations without inventing an individual revocation ruling or sentence.

### Liverman

[DOJ's sentencing announcement](https://www.justice.gov/usao-edva/pr/man-sentenced-5-years-hacking-conspiracy-targeted-senior-us-government-officials) and [his defense counsel's account](https://medvinlaw.com/wikileaks-source-hacking-conspiracy-targeting-senior-u-s-officials-unauthorized-access-to-government-computer-systems-online-accounts-sentenced-to-5-years/) establish the five-year sentence and conspiracy involving computer intrusions, identity theft and harassment. [R v Gamble, paragraph 7](https://www.judiciary.uk/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/r-v-gamble-sentencing.pdf) identifies Liverman as a CWA member and the group's political motivation. It is not described as solely publishing information or participating in an authorized security-testing program. No blanket Anonymous membership is inferred for CWA members.

[The first-party Jeetbot team biography](https://docs.jeetbot.ai/about-jeetbot/team) confirms served federal imprisonment and a post-release career. BOP register **62701-056** gives an actual release of November 22, 2021; its final location is **RRM Raleigh**, a reentry-management location, not proof that the whole term was spent at a prison there. The date is labeled as the end of federal custody in the case narrative. The [September 8, 2016 arrest report](https://www.wect.com/story/33049483/2-nc-men-accused-of-hacking-us-government-computers/) is an arrest date only: intervening release prevents using it as the start of one continuous sentence counter.

### French

[His plea declaration](https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndil/file/797921/dl?inline=) identifies **Timothy Justen French** and a guilty plea to count three, intentionally damaging a protected computer without authorization. [DOJ's November 1, 2016 report](https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndil/pr/member-computer-hacking-group-nullcrew-sentenced-nearly-four-years-launching-cyber) records **45 months imposed**, not four years actually served. [His November 19, 2017 letter](https://pastebin.com/A4yQG46t) confirms substantial actual imprisonment and a recent move to a Manchester, Kentucky halfway house; it also names his earlier Anonymous participation. [Judith Bessant's research](https://www.dhi.ac.uk/san/waysofbeing/data/citizenship-robson-bessant-2016a.pdf), pp.930–931, discusses French/NullCrew within digital dissent and the collective's political campaigns. The conviction is still described as unauthorized computer damage, not simply a protest arrest or a generic DDoS offense.

The current BOP entry for **47606-074** reports release **April 14, 2023**, while earlier supporter notices describe release and renewed custody after the 2017 letter. That 2023 date is **not** attached to the earlier NullCrew case. The later proceedings, custody gaps and legal basis need independent research. No continuous 2014–2023 imprisonment is asserted.

## Identity, missing fields and institutions

- Matched names, aliases and slugs against the 8,786-profile inventory and pending batches 271–279. Fresh query-only live preview found all three missing. The batch repeats matching without global scopes, preserves any single existing match completely, and stops for ambiguity.

- The database contains **USP Victorville**, which is not **FCI Victorville Medium I**. No incorrect facility link is created. French's latest BOP McDowell location is not assigned to an earlier unidentified prison. No new institutions are created.
- Coordinates approximate the documented cities of St. Louis, Morehead City and Morristown. They do not identify private residences or exact detention locations.
- All four cases leave continuous incarceration starts blank where unverified. Thus no exact day/month counter is inferred from sentence length, an arrest, or a later BOP release.
