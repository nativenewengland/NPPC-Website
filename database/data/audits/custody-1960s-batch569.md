# McComb: bail gap and post-trial custody

Hollis Watkins, Bob Moses, Charles McDew. October 4 arrest was followed by bail, documented in the contemporary chronology printed in the Congressional Record (1963 p5846) and Moses firsthand account. The October 31 trial led to later imprisonment (Forman narrative and November 1 Moses jail letter naming Watkins and McDew; corroborating NPS history). Record October 31 as the beginning of the post-trial term, retaining October 4 arrest. Initial pretrial duration remains unresolved and is not silently added to the later term.

Watkins/Hayes signed report explicitly dates their release December 4, 1961; original scan visually inspected. Replace Watkins invented November 12 endpoint, originally derived from a 39-day recollection. Post-trial term is 34 elapsed days. His earlier August case remains separately under review.

Moses and McDew release is supported only to December 1961 here. Do not turn the December 6 press-release publication date into a release date or extend Watkins December 4 date to them. Their month precision prevents a false exact counter. Moses former July 31-December 5 continuous span conflicts with documented bail and separate jail terms. McDew former generic case also referenced Orangeburg 1960; retain that episode as unresolved research, not an implied component of this span. No biographies changed.
