## Evidence and dates

Olive's daughter was interviewed at the May 10, 1980 protest. The report says confinement began in March and ended after a May 8 medical crisis. The 1985 interview independently describes ten weeks and names the Metropolitan Correctional Center. Neither the illness date nor rounded duration is converted into an exact release day. The 1983 subpoena for more exemplars does not establish a second confinement, so none is invented.

Barney's contempt confinement is distinct from his later criminal sentence. His wife's 1985 interview puts prison entry on September 21, 1984 after the three-trial prosecution. The appellate opinion establishes the conspiracy conviction; the later family obituary confirms completed imprisonment. The sources mention Oxford and Danbury; they can reflect different stages, so no single institution relation or transfer date is invented. Frank Durkan's obituary records a June 29 funeral visit, illness and crash the next morning, and death later that day in 1998. This supports June 30, without importing a namesake's birthday or a death-in-custody flag.

Ellis's court record and personal letter agree on February 6, 1982. An official summary says February 8 and a later wire uses February 16 for a collective arrest; those dates are not copied. The letter records March 3, 1983 deportation and arrest at Dublin airport. March precision is retained for the US endpoint because the US departure/handover time is unresolved. No subsequent Irish or British imprisonment is counted. An October 1982 court decision confirms he remained jailed despite bail being set. The alleged passport identities Michael Gilmore and Michael Weir were separately screened against the full snapshot and pending payloads; no normalized collision was found, but no unsupported biographical identity assertion is made.

Carron and Morrison remained detained on January 23 after their January 21 arrests. The official briefing records January 29 deportation; their intervening bail release is not assumed to have occurred on the same day. Both receive January-only release precision. Morrison's biography places return for trial in October 1983; a contemporary verdict report is dated November 9. No exact sentencing day or extra prison term is entered. Probation is excluded from jail time. Sinn Fein activity does not by itself establish formal IRA membership.
