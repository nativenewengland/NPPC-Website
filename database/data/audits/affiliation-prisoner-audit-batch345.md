## Conflicts resolved or retained

- Chinn and Theodis Hewitt: SNCC's February4 summary reports an additional libel conviction. Ed Hollander's signed report explicitly says attorney Carsie Hall reported that count, but neither Hollander nor Claude Weaver, both taking notes in court, recorded it. The February26 report also lists only intimidation. Adopt the verified intimidation count and its$500/six-month sentence; do not silently add the disputed libel count or double the sentence.
- Merritt's sentence line is clipped in February26 scan. Both earlier reports explicitly establish$500/six months per count; sentence sourced there. Original image shows middle name Elaine. No namesake obituary or historian photograph assumed without identity linkage.
- Collier's actual January23–February22 interval exceeds the15-day imposed term; do not overwrite actual endpoints with sentence arithmetic.
- Palmer is not in the January23 list. The dated January28 paragraph continues on page2 and explicitly names his arrest; retain that date.
- February6 WATS lists eight names followed by a separate statement that Watts bonded out that day. This is **not** a February6 release list for the other eight. Their later releases remain February21/22 as individually reported. No sentence-to-release inference.
- C.O.Chinn birth September18,1919 and death July19,1999 follow Becca Walton's Mississippi Encyclopedia biography. Different ages in contemporary reports are not used to calculate birthdays. His brother Clarence and sons Henry Lee/Robert/C.O.Jr are separate identities. The later1970 homicide imprisonment requires its own activism-linkage research and is not automatically added.
