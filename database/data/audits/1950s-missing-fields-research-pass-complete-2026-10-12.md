## Research and preservation
Per-profile source evidence and unresolved findings are in 1950s-missing-fields-progress.json and the numbered 1950s-deep-dive batch audits. Conflicting populated dates are logged without replacement. Indictments, bail orders, verdicts, transfers, pardons, event dates, sightings after release and fixed sentence lengths are not automatically assigned as physical custody endpoints. Ages and namesake vital records are not converted into birthdays. Existing biographies and all other populated fields remain unchanged.
No pull request was created. Exact committed batch files were applied directly on the authorized live server, followed by API cache invalidation and public verification.
