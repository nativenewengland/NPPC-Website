## Sources for additions

Frieda Zucker Katz: https://www.marxists.org/history/usa/pubs/jewish-life/vol-8/v08n10-aug-1954-JL.pdf — Jewish Life, August 1954, printed page 28. Caption identifies Frieda Katz; article identifies her father Louis Zucker and dates her arrest October 6, 1953, and explicitly describes her being taken to jail. Custody endpoint remains unknown; no continuous confinement through trial inferred.

Yvonne Klein: https://www.genekeyes.com/CNVA/GK-NVA-PHOTOS-3.html — image 10 caption identifies back to front Edie Snyder [Maris Arnold], Tyrone Jackson, Yvonne Klein. Yvonne is the foreground woman. Source labels photographer unknown, with Kanaga only a question; no definite photographer attribution made.

Whirlwind Larson: https://www.marxists.org/history/usa/pubs/dailyworker/1935/v12-n087-NAT-apr-11-1935-DW-Q.pdf — Daily Worker, April 11, 1935, page 1 caption identifies A. A. Larson on left; page 2 explicitly equates Whirlwind Larson with A. A. Larson. Right-hand George Novacic excluded from crop.

Torao Uyemura: https://www.fukuimortuary.com/obituaries/tad-uyemura — family obituary identifies Tad Torao Uyemura, born November 22, 1925 (exact match to existing DOB), died May 21, 2022, with portrait. No custody endpoint inferred from death.

Mujahid Farid: https://www.nycourts.gov/reporter/pdfs/2010/2010_33536.pdf — Matter of Farid v Evans, November 12, 2010, PDF page 2 / printed page 1. Register 79-A-0362 matches existing record. Documents January 19, 1979 sentencing for first-degree manslaughter and first-degree attempted murder. Existing sentence summary and year-precision custody endpoints preserved.
