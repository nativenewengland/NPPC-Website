## Date and identity decisions

Nineteen people have explicit transfers, four to Calaca on January12 and fifteen to Manila on February10,1901. Transfer is described in the case but is not stored as release or treated as a new imprisonment. Unknown release dates remain empty, so no exact full term is invented. `in_custody=false` avoids asserting ongoing imprisonment today; `released=false` for an unresolved historical outcome does not mean the person remains jailed. A display that has no dated endpoint may show only the documented start year rather than invent an end.

Diego Rol and Ildefonso Javier have January9 in early rolls and January8 in March. Their capture and incarceration fields therefore use January1901 precision. The March roll records Rol's actual release, but the handwritten day is not resolved confidently; March1901 precision is retained. Sotero Basques has a clear February6 capture and March10 release after a rifle was brought in. Ildefonso Javier remains listed in March without a disposition. Francisco Medrano is listed across January and February, also without a disposition. None of these four receives a February10 transfer inferred from a blank cell. No rank is inferred for Basques from the corporal listed above him.

The Calaca date is January12 in the enlarged original, including the later ditto sequence. The modern transcription's January2 rendering for some entries would precede January9 capture and is not used. Explicit ditto marks are interpreted only within their column; a blank cell is not a ditto mark. Monthly listings are deduplicated into a single identity/case. Name aliases preserve accent, particle and reviewed handwriting variants, including Ropo/Rapo and Gallardo/Guellardo, rather than creating separate identities.

No birthdays are reconstructed from recorded ages. No identity-matched portraits, death dates, precise stockade coordinates or support websites were established. No modern prison ID is assigned to these historical military facilities. Existing records remain untouched.
