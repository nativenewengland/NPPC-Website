## Findings and safeguards

The Midland Reporter-Telegram issue dated Thursday, November 12, 1992 reports Wednesday arrests and gives full names on page 10A. The December 1992 Saoirse page 2 places all four at Otisville after the November 17 bail grant. The later Tucson Weekly interview describes prison visits, subsequent bail for some, and acquittal of all six in April 1994. Therefore the four qualify through actual sustained detention, but neither the bail order nor acquittal is used as their individual release date. No continuous 1992–1994 jail term is asserted. Tucson Six is a case grouping, not a claim of formal IRA membership. Charges remain allegations; the acquittals are explicit.

For Martin, the diplomatic chronology separates two US segments. The sixteen-month sentence is not actual time served. January 31, 1996 is a confirmed transfer into Irish imprisonment. February 18 was his subsequent release from Portlaoise, excluded from US incarceration. Foreign detention and time free between the US segments are excluded.

Identity checks cover full names, component fields, aliases and pending batches. Gerard Anthony Brannigan is supported by the contemporary AP article; the IrishCentral retrospective calls him James, an apparent reporting error. Tony Brannigan is recorded in the University of Arkansas report archived by OJP, printed pages 329–330. That later report describes alleged activity as fact despite the acquittals, so it is used only for the alias lead, not for culpability. Thomas Oliver is distinct from existing Peter Eamon and John David Maguire; Patrick is distinct from Seamus Moley.

No adequate original source for precise birth/death dates or reusable individually identified portraits was found in this pass. Denis Leyne's March 31, 1937 birth and February 5, 1995 death circulate through derivative biographies; original obituary or the cited Covert Entry book remains necessary. The Denver library index gives February 11, 1995 as a newspaper issue date, not a death date. Do not convert contemporary ages into birthdays. No source link is added as a personal-support website.
