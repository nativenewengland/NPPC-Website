# Inmates for Action: next research pass

Research resumed after submitting 100 new IWW identities in PR2494. Batch326 remains unprepared; no new identities counted yet.

Michael Kimble's [2018 remembrance](https://libcom.org/article/black-august-resistance-2018-remembering-inmates-action) names central IFA figures already represented: Richard Lake, Lincoln Heard, George Chagina Dobbins, Tommy Dotson, Anthony Paradise, Frank X. Moore and Charles Beasley. Youngblood, Fleetwood and Mustang require identity resolution. Ordinary imprisonment before political organizing does not by itself establish an activism-related custody episode.

Tom Gardner's [1979 Southern Changes account](https://southernchanges.digitalscholarship.emory.edu/sc01-5_001/sc01-5_006/) identifies Paul Echols and Claude Harris as witnesses describing mistreatment following the 1974 prison uprising. Neither exact name appears in the identity inventory. Determine participation, punishment, case outcome and duration before adding; witness status alone is insufficient.

## Follow-up court review, September11

[Echols v. Sullivan,521F.2d206 (October16,1975)](https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F2/521/521.F2d.206.75-2074.html) says there was no evidence Paul Echols participated in the uprising. He agreed to testify and alleged guard threats; the appellate court remanded for findings rather than deciding those allegations. No separate multi-day punitive custody for his witness activity established. Kenneth Ray Gibbons's appeal was moot after he left Alabama custody; no qualifying activism episode established.

Claude Harris Jr. testified that he had spent21days in segregation before the uprising; the reason was not established as activism. Do not confuse him with the Alabama politician of the same name. Joe Broughton and Burl Moore appear as inmates in testimony; that does not establish activism-related imprisonment. Youngblood, Fleetwood and Mustang remain unresolved identities. No additions from this bounded pass. Queue advanced to garment-workers unions; unresolved leads remain open.
