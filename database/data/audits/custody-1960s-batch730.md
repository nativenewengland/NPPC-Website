# Batch 730: Ralph Allen sentencing month and appeal

Multiple contemporary reports place sentencing in December 1963, contradicting existing November 6 case prose. Exact day disputed: SNCC December 11, News and Letters December 5, other bulletin December 10; record month only. Primary court establishes July 7, 1964 reversal (not July 9 or Supreme Court as some SNCC summaries say). Retain verified initial custody and do not infer additional custody from sentence. Preserve profile biography; its contradictory November 6 wording remains flagged.

SNCC December 1964 release also misstates the sentence as five years; repeated earlier reports identify two years. No extra served duration entered. Biography retained under current date-review scope.
