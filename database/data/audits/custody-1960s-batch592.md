# Custody before sentencing and verified releases

Morton Sobell: Second Circuit establishes August 18, 1950 U.S. arrest and uninterrupted pretrial custody because bail was not posted. April 5, 1951 sentencing is distinct. Sobell v. Reed (1971) independently establishes January 14, 1969 actual release, not merely the appeal date. Earlier seizure in Mexico is not included in this U.S. custody interval.

Harry Gold: Official contemporaneous records distinguish May 22 confession from May 23 formal arrest and remand without bail. December 9, 1950 sentencing confirmed by same-day FBI memorandum. AP release photograph and contemporary Kingsport report reprinted in Congressional Record establish May 18, 1966 release, not parole approval in April.

David Greenglass: United States v. Rosenberg 108 F.Supp.798 recounts Greenglass testimony of June 15, 1950 arrest and removal to FBI offices. FBI history gives June 16 formal complaint, arraignment and remand; use initial physical custody June 15. Congressional chronology gives April 6, 1951 sentencing. Same-day AP report November 16, 1960 establishes actual release at 8:35 a.m.
