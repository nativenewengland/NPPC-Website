# Batch 698: Bruce Dancis custody endpoints

Dancis dated his surrender and initial county-jail confinement May 20, 1969 in his July 1971 first-person account. Lew Zipin, a contemporary fellow activist reviewing the memoir, explicitly gives May 20, 1969 through December 23, 1970. The memoir chapter abstract also identifies December 23 parole, although its later December 3 wording appears inconsistent. Preserve the separate November 1968 week of custody as unresolved rather than extending continuous custody through bail.

Changes: incarceration May 20, 1969; parole December 23, 1970. May 29 was transfer into Ashland, not initial confinement. Existing 19 documented months, biography and unrelated fields preserved. The earlier 1968 bail episode still needs exact dates.
