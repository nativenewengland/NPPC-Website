## Evidence and precision

- **Trimiew, 1993:** [The Physiologist, December 1993](https://www.physiology.org/docs/default-source/archive_tphys/the-physiologist-newsletter-1993-december.pdf?sfvrsn=52445d8c_2), printed p.240/PDF p.34, reports actual custody and October 20 release. The [October 20 bail order](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/9/1388/540924/) and [November 12 merits decision](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/9/1389/541029/) are different rulings. Bail was not an acquittal; August subpoena/refusal dates are not demonstrated confinement dates.
- **Stout and Trimiew, 1994:** [March 28 appellate decision](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/21/1113/623084/) and [Animal People, June 1994 court calendar](https://newspaper.animalpeopleforum.org/1994/06/01/court-calendar-52/). The latter's “Kim Trimview” is matched to the court's Kimberly Jeannine Trimiew. The possible April 1995 grand-jury endpoint is a prospective limit, not a release date. [Memories of Freedom](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/western-wildlife-unit-of-the-animal-liberation-front-memories-of-freedom), chapters 6–7, identifies the Coalition Against Fur Farms and the two volunteers. Its collective duration language does not establish individual exact endpoints.
- **Lynn, 1999:** [Her own Earth First! account, Samhain 1999](https://www.environmentandsociety.org/sites/default/files/key_docs/ef_20_1_1.pdf), printed p.12/PDF p.13. The September 13 release resolves “Monday” immediately after the dated Friday, September 10 arrest; [September 13 contemporary reporting](https://www.sfgate.com/bayarea/article/ALAMEDA-COUNTY-Animal-Rights-Activist-Starts-2908869.php) corroborates that weekend. Her October account describes a new confinement in Clayton, Missouri. Do not count the intervening release as imprisonment.
- **Lynn, 2004:** [Earth First!, November–December 2004](https://www.environmentandsociety.org/sites/default/files/key_docs/ef_25_1_3.pdf), printed p.13/PDF p.16, explicitly dates confinement August 26 and describes release at a hearing on the 23rd day. [Seattle Post-Intelligencer release reporting](https://www.seattlepi.com/seattlenews/article/animal-rights-activist-released-1154498.php) confirms SeaTac and approximately three weeks. Its September 17 archive header with “yesterday” conflicts with the journal's ordinal day. Exact release day therefore remains unset.
- **Berardi:** [Seattle Post-Intelligencer, August 25, 2004](https://www.seattlepi.com/seattlenews/article/animal-rights-activist-rebuffs-federal-grand-jury-1152552.php), establishes several days at SeaTac the preceding month. Exact endpoints require the original July notices.

All three newspaper PDF source pages were visually inspected, including Lynn's named portrait caption. Source identifiers and evidence are retained for each separate episode in `batch279.json`.
