Its February 22, 1985 train blockade ended in acquittal; a possible sentence is not time served. The February 2020 memorial date is not a verified death date. Retain this lead without creating a prisoner entry yet.

[Reed's recording inventory](https://archivesspace.reed.edu/repositories/2/top_containers/1843?page=3) identifies a March 31, 1970 talk about prison experiences by Robert Wollheim and Selah Chamberlain (object 541). Together with [Wollheim's college obituary](https://www.reed.edu/reed-magazine/in-memoriam/obituaries/2020/robert-douglas-wollheim-70.html), it supports further research into Chamberlain. The recording itself has not been reviewed; its event date is not a release date, and no individual duration is established here.
