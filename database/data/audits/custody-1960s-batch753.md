# Batch 753: two confirmed Freedom Rider jail admissions

Etta Marie Simpson

The archival arrest record supplies May 28; fellow prisoner Mary Jean Smith identifies Simpson in her jail diary, and interview reporting confirms imprisonment following that arrest. Records initial Jackson custody, not a Parchman admission date. No exact release inferred.

Gwendolyn C. Jenkins

Big Spring Daily Herald, June 8, 1961, page 1, AP report names Gwendolyn Jenkins among the three St. Louis airport riders arrested Wednesday and reports the nine new riders held in Jackson jails Thursday. Original scan inspected. This corroborates immediate June 7 custody; release remains unknown.
