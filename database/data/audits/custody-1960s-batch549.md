# Dick Gregory later fish-in sentence service

Later service of the fish-in sentence, after appeals. Began in June 1968; sources disagree between June 6 and June 7, so only the month is recorded. Released July 16, 1968 as a trustee at large to serve the remainder outside jail. This endpoint is release from physical confinement, not completion of the 90-day sentence. His hospital stay during the fast remained guarded custody. The February 1966 pretrial detention is recorded separately.

HistoryLink dates physical release July16; contemporary Helix volume4 issue2 p21 confirms trustee-at-large release on Tuesday and guarded hospital custody. June admission day conflicts, so retain month precision and do not calculate an exact total or count the full imposed90days.
