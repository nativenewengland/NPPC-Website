# Batch 699: four antiwar custody records

## Les Bayless

May 21, 1969 detention; four years served; 1973 release. Congressional Record separately establishes February 16, 1970 sentencing. Exact release day unresolved.

## Michael Bransome

Archival arrest photo establishes May 21, 1969 arrest. His June 1970 letter dates sentencing September 1969; Congressional Record confirms nolo contendere. Furlough/escape is not formal release; initial detention continuity and endpoint require further research. Existing eight documented months retained.

## Gary Rader

Contemporary movement report explicitly dates arrest April 18; TIME reports overnight Chicago jail and next-day bail. The resulting April 19 endpoint is calendar resolution of next-day wording. Separate Pentagon case unchanged.

- https://www.crmvet.org/docs/670400_mobe_spring.pdf
- https://time.com/archive/6890332/protest-burning-issue/

## Barry Bondhus

February 25, 1966 Minneapolis Star report, reprinted in Rebel Worker 6, establishes arrest by that date, after the Wednesday action. May Fifth Estate reports intervening bail. Record only February arrest month; do not conflate it with the later prison term or overwrite conflicting 14/18-month duration accounts.

- https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/rebel-worker-group-rebel-worker-6
- https://www.fifthestate.org/archive/8-may-1966/vietnam-newsletter/
