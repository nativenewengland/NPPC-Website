The entry uses the sheriff's tentative allegation and FOX6's distinction for Brieno, and records only the verified April detention. No codefendant's charges, sentence or formal group membership is assigned to her.

Durand: the court establishes three trespass counts, acquittals and the later sentencing reversal. The interview establishes time served and release. The2009 appellate ruling did not vacate his trespass convictions. Precise custody endpoints and final resentencing remain open.
