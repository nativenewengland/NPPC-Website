# Batch 320: Spokane free-speech prisoners

Three missing prisoners, three cases and three sourced death dates. Prepared locally; no production data changed. Existing profiles, biographies, cases, photos and support websites are preserved in full.

| Person | Evidence | Dates and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Samuel O. Chinn / S. O. Chinn | Contemporary obituary describes more than a month in jail, followed by release and illness | Death March 18, 1910. Reports differ between 30, 35 and 45 days; no exact total or entry/release date invented |
| Henry Bordet | January 26, 1911 local appeal explicitly reports thirty days on bread and water in Spokane jail and subsequent hospital treatment | Death February 7, 1911, stated in the signed Local 437 resolution published February 23. UW lists February 1; the contemporary resolution takes precedence |
| F. J. Ferry / F. Ferry | Contemporary newspapers describe thirty-four days at Franklin School and illness after release | Arrest and custody year 1909 only. Death April 7, 1910: April 8 Spokane Press says last night; UW agrees. April 16 Industrial Worker instead says April 5, which is not adopted |

Chinn and Bordet custody belongs to the 1909–1910 campaign; exact starting year is not forced from the campaign dates or the phrase a year ago. No birth year inferred from reported ages. No death after release mislabeled as death in custody. Medical causation is attributed to the contemporary account, not asserted as an independently verified finding. No verified individual photographs found in the reviewed sources. A reproduction of Ferry death certificate was located but could not be retrieved (406); it is not claimed as inspected evidence.
