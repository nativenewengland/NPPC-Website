# 1990s vital dates and photographs — September 24, 2026

This pass screened live profiles assigned to the 1990s era or connected to a case with a 1990–1999 arrest or incarceration date. The initial queue contained 273 profiles with at least one blank birth date, death date, or photograph.

## Published additions

Nine live profiles received corroborated updates:

- **Hasan Shakur (Derrick Wayne Frazier):** exact birth date and official Texas Department of Criminal Justice portrait.
- **Peter Dougherty:** exact birth date from his Jamnalal Bajaj Foundation award biography.
- **Ramsey Muñiz:** exact death date from his funeral-home notice and Texas Public Radio.
- **Aimee Allison:** portrait from her official biography, credited to Justin Buell.
- **Yolanda Huet-Vaughn:** identified portrait crop from a Physicians for a National Health Program photograph by Mark Almberg.
- **Helen Woodson:** corrected exact birth date and added exact death date from the Nuclear Resister, independently supported by the National Catholic Reporter.
- **Dan Sage:** exact death date and obituary portrait from the Syracuse Post-Standard. The source image's large white border was removed.
- **Clarence Davis:** exact death date from a memorial by Gulf War resistance organizer and friend Betsy Esch.
- **Rita Steinhagen:** identified crop from Michael J. Bayly's photograph of the November 1997 School of the Americas protest.

Five photographs, two birth dates, four death dates, and one correction to an existing birth date were published. Every added image is a crop or resize of the cited source image; no AI image editing was used.

## Remaining screening queue

The final inventory contains 269 profiles with at least one blank field:

- 193 have no supported birth date.
- 264 have no death date recorded.
- 192 have no photograph.

A blank death date is expected for a person who is alive and is not itself evidence of missing research. Age statements that only narrow a birth to two possible calendar years were not converted into a birth date. For example, the available sources place Dan Sage's birth between March 24, 1927 and January 12, 1928, so his birth field remains blank rather than assigning an unsupported year.

The full final queue is saved in `inventory-final.json`; source-by-source changes are recorded in `specs.json`.
