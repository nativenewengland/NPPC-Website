## Book and interview research added at the user’s request

Prioritize these broad sources while continuing Catholic Worker research:

1. Arthur J. Laffin, [Swords Into Plowshares, Volume Two: A Chronology of Plowshares Disarmament Actions, 1980–2003](https://wipfandstock.com/9781608990511/swords-into-plowshares-volume-two/). Action-by-action coverage with trials, sentences and some participant photographs. Its scope is Plowshares, including overseas actions, not every Catholic Worker action. Publisher listing/catalogue checked; full book not yet accessed.
2. Rosalie G. Riegle, [Doing Time for Peace: Resistance, Family, and Community](https://www.vanderbiltuniversitypress.com/9780826518729/doing-time-for-peace/). More than75 narrators, with a Catholic Worker section and actual imprisonment accounts. Family-member interviews must not be presumed prisoner records.
3. Riegle, [Crossing the Line: Nonviolent Resisters Speak Out for Peace](https://wipfandstock.com/9781610976831/crossing-the-line/). More than65 narrators, actions, trials and imprisonment, including non-US cases that need filtering.
4. Rosalie Riegle Troester, [Voices from the Catholic Worker](https://catholicworker.org/books/voices-from-the-catholic-worker/), 1993. Over200 interviews about the broader movement; war resistance is one strand, not a universal custody roster.
5. [Marquette Peace Prisoners Oral History Collection](https://www.marquette.edu/library/archives/Mss/ppoh/index.php), the source interviews behind the two prison books. Public [transcript inventory](https://www.marquette.edu/library/archives/Mss/ppoh/ppoh-series1.php) saved locally:144 folder entries. This count includes repeated/joint interviews and relatives, not144 eligible missing prisoners. Collection states transcripts are available to on-site researchers; an online finding aid is not access to their full contents. No archive contact or purchase made.

The [Catholic Worker review of A Ministry of Risk](https://catholicworker.org/stop-the-killing-a-review-of-philip-berrigans-a-ministry-of-risk/) links both a Plowshares chronology and Art Laffin’s history, offering public research routes. No single source checked so far establishes exhaustive coverage of every Catholic Worker action.
