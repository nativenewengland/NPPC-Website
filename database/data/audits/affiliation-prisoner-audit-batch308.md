## Date conflicts and precision

- Jennie Miller and Kate Koppa: twenty days in the June 15 roster versus ten days in the thirteen-person release account. Both retained as attributed reporting; no numerical duration.
- Evelyn Dornfield: ten-day June accounts versus thirty-day July release account. Release year only; the month is unresolved.
- Victoria Cieslakiewicz: forty-five days in June versus sixty days in the August 1 release report. July release month is supported by the latter; exact day and numerical duration withheld.
- Eleanor Sadlowski: June account gives forty-five days; an August 9 search lead gives sixty. The latter remains an unreviewed lead and is not used to set a release date.
- Lena Movich, Vanda Kaleto, Eleanor Sadlowski: scheduled release is not actual release. No release endpoint supplied.
- No thirty-day sentence has been converted into one calendar month. No date is calculated by subtracting a term from a newspaper publication date.
