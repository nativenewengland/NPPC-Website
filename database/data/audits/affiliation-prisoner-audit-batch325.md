## Evidence and date boundaries

The IWW magazine names the detainees sent to Ellis Island in February 1919. Its arrival date, February 11, is independently corroborated by the contemporary March 22, 1919 Voice of Labour report, which describes incommunicado detention and subsequent legal proceedings. These are sustained immigration-custody cases, not an arrest-only list or invented criminal convictions.

The incarceration field records the **documented Ellis Island admission on February 11, 1919**. It is not the date of the original arrest or the start of all US detention. Earlier custody and travel under guard are excluded from the dated segment. For unknown-release cases, there is no invented endpoint, completed duration or present-day custody flag. These records document a minimum known segment, not necessarily the whole imprisonment.

The release list follows the explicitly dated 1919 transfer; its March-April dates are entered in 1919, not the magazine publication year 1920. Releases are not described as deportations or criminal acquittals. The duplicated typography around April 22 is not counted as additional people. Source initials remain initials.
