National Guardian June 13, 1960 reports he had been jailed and spoke in New York June 4, corroborating release before that appearance. Month precision used because the Register day is not visually verified; no group release day or retrospective publication day is assigned. Exact release unresolved.

## Martin Chancey

Contemporaneous United States Attorneys Bulletin volume 1 number 9 printed page 8 explicitly dates Martin Chancey arrest at Pittsburgh November 4, 1953, in the Smith Act prosecution already described by this profile. Original scan visually confirms 4; OCR incorrectly reads 11. This is the earlier prosecution, not a fabricated 1961 HUAC arrest. Admission and release remain unverified.
