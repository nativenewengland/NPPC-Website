# Batch 263 — 100 portrait previews and credits

100 missing portraits: 66 National Archives prison photographs, 33 named photographs in Labor Defender (1926 and 1930), and one Bain News Service photograph from the Library of Congress. Existing photos, biographies, cases, dates and support websites are preserved.

The user authorized exact rectangular crops. These PNGs retain the original pixels, including grain, fading and historical printing. Scans may receive a lossless right-angle rotation before cropping. No AI reconstruction or retouching. [Original files and identifying captions](batch263-originals/) are retained.
