# Recent-profile photograph pass — September 30, 2026

This pass reviewed the prisoner profiles created during the preceding 24 hours that had no photograph. Each included image was matched to a named subject by the source caption or an official identification. Images are cropped to a consistent 4:5 profile format; the original source and credit are recorded in `manifest.json`.

Thirteen profile photographs were accepted. Candidate images for Catherine Beth Washburn, Gregory Vandenberg, and other recent profiles were not used because they showed evidence, a distant or obscured figure, or did not permit a reliable individual match.
