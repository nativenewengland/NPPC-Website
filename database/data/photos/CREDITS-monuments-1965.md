# National-monuments defendants: batch 249

The three JPEGs below are portrait extracts from the Associated Press wirephoto
captioned **Charged With Bomb Plot On National Shrines**, published on page 1 of
the **Big Spring Daily Herald, February 17, 1965**. The scan is held by Texas Tech
University Libraries, Southwest Collection/Special Collections Library.
