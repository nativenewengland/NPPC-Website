# C. T. Vivian portrait (batch 250)

`c-t-vivian-doe.jpg` is the existing 540 × 768 portrait extract of a photograph
by **Ken Shipp / U.S. Department of Energy** at a Martin Luther King Jr. Day
celebration at DOE headquarters. Commons identifies the work as public domain
(`PD US DOE`). The downloaded portrait is unchanged.

- [Portrait file and rights metadata](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Photo_by_Ken_Shipp-DOE_Photo_(6853349205)_(Ausschnitt).jpg)
- [Original DOE photograph](https://www.flickr.com/photos/departmentofenergy/6853349205/)
- [Original photograph on Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Photo_by_Ken_Shipp-DOE_Photo_(6853349205).jpg)

The original caption identifies Rev. C. T. Vivian as the second person from
the left. The portrait was visually compared with that position in the original
on September 7, 2026. The original scan has a damaged lower portion; that portion
is outside the existing portrait extract. No restoration or generated imagery
was used. Commons API image metadata supplied the download and rights details
because the file description did not render in the web reader. Its author/date
fields on the derivative are malformed; the credit follows the original DOE
photograph. No exposure date is inferred from the derivative upload date.
