The caption identifies him second from left, between Stanley Rappeport and Albert

of the entire newspaper page; a standalone portrait is not included. His photo
field remains empty. No unrelated modern Schoenfeld portrait is substituted.

The batch copies bundled files into the public storage disk and sets photo paths
only for newly created profiles. It verifies file signatures and SHA-256 hashes,
and refuses to overwrite a different existing file. Upload-directory dates on
source websites are not treated as dates the photographs were taken.
