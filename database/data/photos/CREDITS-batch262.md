# Batch 262 — 100 portrait previews and credits

One hundred additional portraits for existing prisoners with empty photo fields, sourced from individually named National Archives at Kansas City records. These are federal Leavenworth booking photographs, mainly of World War I labor organizers and antiwar prisoners. All selected photographs were visually checked against the catalog names and inmate numbers.

The user authorized exact rectangular crops. Each PNG preserves the retained pixels of the frontal photograph on the right. Original grain, fading, stains and inscriptions remain. The [original files and catalog metadata](batch262-originals/) are retained for review. No biographies, cases, dates, support websites or existing photos are changed.

NARA marks the selected photographic items as Unrestricted for both use and access. These federal-government booking photographs are public domain in the United States. This statement concerns the photographic items; some parent case files carry separate access restrictions.
