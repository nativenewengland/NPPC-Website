# Batch 260 — 50 portrait previews and credits

Fifty additional missing-photo assignments, researched September 8, 2026. Each selected source image was visually inspected. The original downloaded files are retained unchanged, including source-side crops, historical edits and provider-generated thumbnails. No new pixel edits or AI reconstruction were made. Small and grainy historical portraits remain small and grainy.

Rights vary by image. Public-domain and Creative Commons labels below follow the linked archive/file records. Five biographical images have no open reuse license; they are explicitly identified rather than represented as public domain. James Omura has a noncommercial share-alike license. These credits do not grant blanket permission for merchandise or unrelated reuse.
